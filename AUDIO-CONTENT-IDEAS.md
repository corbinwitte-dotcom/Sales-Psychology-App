## Recording Tips

### Structure (Psycho-Cybernetics Based)
1. **Relaxation Phase** (2-3 min)
   - Progressive muscle relaxation
   - Breathing exercises
   - Calm the mind

2. **Visualization Phase** (4-8 min)
   - Vivid, specific scenarios
   - First-person perspective
   - Engage all senses
   - Include emotions

3. **Affirmation Phase** (1-2 min)
   - Positive, present-tense statements
   - Identity-based ("I am...")
   - Confident delivery

4. **Return Phase** (1 min)
   - Gentle return to awareness
   - Carry confidence forward
   - Ready for action

### Voice & Delivery
- **Pace**: Slower than normal speech
- **Tone**: Calm, confident, reassuring
- **Volume**: Steady and clear
- **Pauses**: Use silence for processing
- **Music**: Optional soft background (if licensed)

### Best Practices
- Use second-person ("You see yourself...")
- Be specific with details
- Focus on feelings and emotions
- Use present tense
- Include success outcomes
- Keep it positive (what you want, not what you don't want)

## Content Length Guidelines

- **Quick Prep** (3-5 min): Right before an event
- **Standard Session** (7-10 min): Daily practice
- **Deep Work** (12-15 min): Weekly intensive
- **Full Program** (20-30 min): Special occasions

## Recording Equipment

**Budget Setup:**
- USB Microphone (Blue Yeti, Audio-Technica AT2020USB+)
- Quiet room with soft furnishings
- Free software: Audacity

**Professional Setup:**
- XLR Microphone (Shure SM7B, Rode NT1)
- Audio Interface (Focusrite Scarlett)
- DAW: Adobe Audition, Logic Pro
- Acoustic treatment

## Rights & Licensing
